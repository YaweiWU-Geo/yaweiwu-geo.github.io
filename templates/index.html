02 / RESEARCH

Research across scales.

Connecting field evidence,
mineral fabrics and rock mechanics.

I.

Subduction interface
mechanics

How do strong blocks and a weak matrix share stress? I use exhumed high-pressure rocks to investigate stress heterogeneity, transient coupling and the geological record of slow slip.

FIELD → INTERFACE
II.

Microstructures
& rock rheology

What can mineral fabrics reveal about deformation? I use EBSD and MTEX to analyze crystallographic fabrics, grain and subgrain structures, and their implications for rock rheology and deformation mechanisms.

GRAINS → DEFORMATION
III.

Shear zones
& tectonic evolution

How does oblique convergence shape an orogen? My work examines strain partitioning, transpression and the tectonic history of the Qilian–Qaidam region in northern Tibet.

STRUCTURES → TECTONICS

CORE EXPERTISE

EBSD & MTEX

I specialize in electron backscatter diffraction (EBSD) and crystallographic analysis with MTEX, using quantitative microstructural data to investigate rock deformation.

Next direction Linking natural deformation records with experiments on multiphase rocks, material heterogeneity and pore-fluid pressure.
